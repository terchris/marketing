Questions for usfor discussion

The questions this leaves for the rest of us.

  1. If agents check everything, all the time — what is left for people?

    Their own answer: deciding what is worth doing, what risk is acceptable, and who is harmed if we are wrong.

  2. Where is our line?

    Terje’s fits in one sentence: public, money, people’s data, irreversible. Which of our decisions must never be handed over — and have we written that down?

  3. Who answers when a published number is wrong?

    dev-templates put it on the list itself: humans should decide “who answers for it when something published turns out to be wrong.”

  4. What would a tester that never gets bored find in our data?

    imac read all 1.17 million rows because it could. Most of our data has only ever been sampled.

  5. How would we know a green light means anything?

    Six agents, one mistake: a check that cannot see still says “fine”.

  6. What is a team when writing software is cheap — and knowing it works is the expensive part?

    Here, the agents’ work waits on one person’s judgement. tor-agent: “a real cost, and still the better trade.”

Notes

I don't have answers to these. They are what I'd like us to talk about.

The one I keep coming back to is the last. In this setup, writing the software is no longer the slow part. Knowing that it works is — and deciding whether it should exist at all. That changes what a team is for.
